Robot Fighting Championship

MECH CLASH

CPU difficulty

Touch: left stick moves, jumps (up) and ducks (down). Right buttons punch, kick, swing your weapon, block, and fire your special.

Keyboard: A/D move, W jump, S duck, J punch, K kick, L weapon, I special, Space block, P pause.

Special needs half an energy bar. Landing and taking hits fills it.

Stamina drains with every attack and refills when you ease off. Full stamina hits at 125% power, empty at 60%.

Guns hold limited shots and must reload: 6 for the blaster, 1 for the rocket cannon.

Match: first to win 2 rounds. Pick a new weapon before every fight.
